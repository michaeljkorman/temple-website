✦ The Laws of the Temple ✦

The temple is a living organism—a machine made of gates. To enter, one must act: knock, offer, or acquire:

  1. Knock on the Gate of Intent, or
  2. Make an offering of $11, or
  3. Acquire an item from the temple shop.

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

These acts do NOT grant entry. They leave one in the courtyard:

  1. Commenting.
  2. Sending messages.
  3. Liking, subscribing, or following.

(Those who wish to stay in the courtyard, outside the temple, must obey the Courtyard Decrees.)

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

The temple contains:

  • Rites of distortion and stillness, both to be experienced, not completed.
  • Parables and lore that hold the temple’s history and illustrate its design.
  • The Fragmented Scrolls, a founding document.
  • A sacred tract.
  • Spoken discourses
  • Temple bucks—a sacred currency
  • The ritual hall—the canonical stream of drops.
  • The reels and shorts: mere noise in the courtyard.
  • The back room and the secret corridor.

Those who wish to simply experience the temple, to step into its rhythm and let it shape them, will find a Gate here as well.

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

The temple is not a cult, not a game, not a brand.

It is a force, born in strange times, when the promise of what could be hung in the air.

It calls only to those who Create.
Those who do not will feel the friction of its gates.

To enter the myth, one must find the Gate.

the purpose of art is not the release of a momentary ejection of adrenaline but is, rather, the gradual, lifelong construction of a state of wonder and serenity
