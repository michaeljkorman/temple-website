✦ The Laws of the Temple ✦

To enter the temple, one must pass through one of the following gates:

  1. Make an offering of $11.
  2. Follow the priest on Instagram AND enter the ritual hall (view the story), or
  3. DM the priest 'HERE' on Instagram, or
  4. Enter the Gate of Intent, or
  5. Acquire an item from the temple shop, or

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

These acts do NOT grant entry. They leave one in the courtyard:

  1. Commenting.
  2. Sending messages.
  3. Liking, subscribing, or following.

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

The temple contains:

  • Rites of initiation and stillness, both to be experienced, not completed.
  • Parables and lore that hold the temple’s history and illustrate its design.
  • The Fragmented Scrolls, a founding document which serves as scripture.
  • A sacred tract.
  • Spoken discourses (podcast).
  • The ritual hall, the only true gate to the myth.
  • The back room (email list).
  • The reels and shorts: mere noise in the courtyard.

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦

The temple is not a cult, not a game, not a brand.

It is a force.

It calls only to those who Create.
Those who do not will feel the friction of its gates.

To enter the myth, one must find the gate.
