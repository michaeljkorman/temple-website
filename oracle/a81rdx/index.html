Ah — now we’re talking about practical leverage from a viewer/fan perspective, which is a different mindset than analyzing him as a puzzling personality. Let’s break it down carefully.

1. Understand his priorities first

Based on everything you’ve observed, he is not motivated primarily by fame or money:

  • He values control over perception and intentionality.
  • He gets frustrated when casual attention or misinterpretation creeps in.
  • His creative process is like a delicate experiment: the artifacts, rituals, and audience are all variables.

This is key: trying to “push him toward success” in the conventional sense (mass audience, ad revenue, viral growth) without respecting that could backfire.

2. What does work as leverage

Since he wants intentional, controlled engagement, leverage comes from alignment with those goals rather than blunt amplification.

A) Focused, meaningful engagement

  • Participate in the project in ways that match his ritualized framework:
    • Pay the $11 Gate of Intent (if it exists)
    • Engage with artifacts or documentation seriously
    • Leave thoughtful comments that show attention and comprehension

This signals to him that the project can attract committed participants, which validates his system without causing chaos.

B) Amplify quality attention, not raw volume

  • Introduce him to audiences who will value depth over quantity: people who appreciate the ritual, font, or music seriously.
  • Share content with communities that will treat it intentionally rather than as meme fodder.

C) Protect perception / reduce noise

  • Avoid spreading it casually to audiences that might misinterpret or troll.
  • Help curate or moderate engagement if that’s ever possible.

D) Provide tools, not just attention

  • If you have technical, organizational, or social skills, you could help him:
    • Document the system better (like FAQs, guides, or visualizations)
    • Structure audience participation safely
    • Maintain platforms (GitHub, websites) so his experimental infrastructure is stable

This is more leveraged support than just watching or subscribing.

3. Why traditional “more views, more ads, more subs” may backfire

  • More casual attention increases friction and stress for him.
  • He may interpret mass audience as chaos, not validation.
  • The Temple system works best with a small, attentive, intentional audience.

So “success” for him may look very different from “success” in YouTube terms:

A small, engaged, serious group that participates meaningfully and sustains the system.

4. Summary: How to maximize your support

  • Participate intentionally
  • Signal understanding and attention
  • Introduce him to quality, aligned audiences
  • Offer tools and infrastructure that reduce chaos
  • Avoid trying to push mass exposure if it risks destabilizing the system
