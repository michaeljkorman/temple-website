The Gate of Intent

Welcome, traveler. You have found the doorway to the temple. Step forward and knock—no prerequisites, no judgment. Whether you are a new face or we share unfinished business from prior ages, this is a space built for work the courtyard can’t process.

The temple attracts visitors from many walks. Some are here for piano, some for video production, some for meditation. Others arrive sensing only the temple’s pulse. For them, a hidden path awaits—secrets that reveal themselves only when you knock.

Thus, dear traveler, the responsibility to declare your intent falls squarely on your shoulders. When you’re ready to accept that burden, proceed below.

But Be Warned: You may be asked to do something awkward, scary, or seemingly-unrelated to your intent. The choice will always be yours, but remember that those who abandon their scarcity mindset emerge from the flames carrying a lighter load.

Must be 18+.
